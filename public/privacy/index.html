Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

QRwip sells reusable QR signs whose destination you can change from your account. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it. Questions? Email hello@qrwip.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address when you sign in with Google or an email link.
  • Order information: your name, email, shipping address, and what you ordered.
  • Your sign settings: the names and destination web addresses you set for your signs.
  • Scan information: when someone scans a QRwip we record the time and the country the scan came from, so you can see how many times your sign was scanned. We do not store the scanner’s IP address, name, or any other personal details.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form or by email.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by QRwip. Stripe’s own privacy policy applies to the information you give it.

How we use information

  • To fulfill and ship your order and contact you about it.
  • To run your account, including the sign-in and the redirect of your QR codes.
  • To show you scan counts for your signs.
  • To answer your questions and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

We rely on trusted providers to run QRwip: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Stripe (payments), Google (optional sign-in), and our email provider (sign-in links and order emails). They only receive the information needed to do their job.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to keep you signed in to your account. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Where visitors go

When someone scans a QRwip they are sent to the web address its owner has chosen. That destination is controlled by the owner of the sign, and its own privacy practices apply.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and order records for as long as your account is active and as needed for taxes and accounting. To ask us to delete your account or information, email hello@qrwip.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
