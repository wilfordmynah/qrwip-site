Got questions? We got answers.

Can I change where my QRwip sends people?

Yes. That's the whole idea. Your physical QRwip stays the same while the destination changes whenever you need it to.

Do I or my visitors need an app?

No. Visitors scan with their phone's regular camera, like any other QR code.

Can I use QRwip for something besides real estate?

Yes. We're starting with real estate, but QRwip works anywhere a reusable physical QR code makes sense.

Can I order more than one?

Yes, and the more you order, the less each one costs: 10% off at 5 or more, 15% off at 10 or more, and 20% off at 25 or more. Need QRwips for a whole team or brokerage? Contact us.

Can I get a custom design?

Yes. We can put your logo and brand colors on the sign. Custom designs take a little longer than standard signs, and we'll send you a proof to approve before printing. Contact us for a quote.

How do I change my QRwip's destination?

Email us the new link and we'll update it for you, without touching the physical sign. A dashboard where you can change it yourself is on the way.
